# The one screen that has to work
_(DRAFT 2026-07-11 — counterpart session's best statement of the core screen from the vault record. Owning session + Cole: sharpen against the live UI, then remove this line.)_

**The screen:** the passage workspace — the pastor's text on screen, being broken into phrases and arranged into a diagram that exposes the passage's structure.

**Who opens it:** a pastor, Monday, coffee, sermon due Sunday, passage chosen.

**What they see:** the passage text, ready to be worked — not a dashboard, not settings, not a library. The text is the interface.

**What they do:** split the text into phrases; indent/arrange phrases to show relationships (main clauses vs. modifiers); watch the argument of the passage become visible; carry that structure into their sermon prep.

**The test:** if a first-time user with a real passage and a real deadline can reach "oh — THAT'S how this text is built" on this screen, the product works. Everything else in the app exists to serve that moment or get out of its way.
