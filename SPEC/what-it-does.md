# What it does — one page
_(DRAFT 2026-07-11, assembled from the vault's record of Cole's intent by the counterpart session. Owning build session + Cole: correct anything that has drifted from the live product, then remove this line.)_

**The product:** a desktop app that walks a pastor from a raw passage to a preachable understanding of it — text-driven exegesis with phrasing diagrams, keeping the passage in its proper context the whole way.

**Who it's for:** under-resourced pastors and serious teachers — people preaching weekly without seminary-level language tools or a study team. The COVENANT method's audience.

**The job it's hired to do:** Monday morning, sermon due Sunday. The pastor knows the passage but not yet its argument. The app gets them from "I have a text" to "I see how this text works and what it's actually claiming" — faster and more faithfully than the commentary-skimming they'd otherwise do.

**What "working" means:**
- A pastor can open a passage and produce a phrasing diagram of it without training beyond a first-use walkthrough.
- The flow enforces context-first habits (the method is the moat — it teaches while it tools).
- Output is usable in real prep: exportable/printable into their manuscript workflow.

**What this is NOT (v1):**
- Not a sermon writer. It prepares the preacher; it doesn't preach.
- Not a commentary library or search engine.
- Not the specialist-agents chat experience (future, recorded in STACK.md).

**Why it exists strategically:** the spirit/mind pillar of the BASE 1520 app vision; the software expression of the SOM/COVENANT hermeneutic; a product an under-served market (most pastors have no such tool priced for them) can actually buy.
