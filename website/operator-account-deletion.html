Delete Your Account

The Operator gives you an in-app deletion path and a web request path. You do not have to reinstall the app to request deletion.

FASTEST PATH

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Account.
  2. Scroll to Delete Account.
  3. Review any active-subscription warning.
  4. Choose Permanently Delete.

The Operator removes the account and signed-in data immediately after confirmation.

NO APP ACCESS?

Request deletion here

Email us from the address attached to your Operator account. Put Delete My Operator Account in the subject. We may reply once to verify ownership before deleting the account.

WHAT HAPPENS

Data deleted and data retained

  • Deleted: account email, authentication devices, synced studies, study documents, questions, notes, and account-linked download records.
  • Marketing: the matching Mailchimp contact is archived when configured.
  • Retained without account identity: anonymized token-and-cost records may remain so BASE1520 can reconcile service expenses.
  • Retained for abuse prevention: keyed one-way values derived from the email and install identifier may remain solely to prevent repeated free-trial claims. They are not stored as readable email addresses or device IDs.
  • Legal and payment records: limited transaction records may be retained when required for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, or law.
Subscriptions are separate. Deleting the account does not let BASE1520 cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription for you. Cancel store billing in your Apple or Google account. A Stripe subscription requested through the web will be handled during the verified deletion process.

Verified web requests are ordinarily completed within 30 days. Questions may be sent to info@base1520.com.
