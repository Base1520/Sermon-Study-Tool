Product-Specific Notice

The Operator

The Operator is BASE1520's Scripture-study application. When you use it, BASE1520 may process the following information:

  • Account and device data: email address, an internal account identifier, a randomly generated install identifier, linked-device records, authentication-token hashes, basic platform information, and limited account-recovery records such as request time, expiration, attempt count, and keyed one-way code and install hashes. A recovery code is not stored in readable form.
  • Study and search content: Bible references and passage searches, generated study documents, questions, notes, and the content needed to answer a study request.
  • Purchase and entitlement data: subscription product, provider, status, renewal period, and signed purchase evidence from Apple, Google, or Stripe. BASE1520 does not receive a full payment-card number from those providers.
  • Service usage: studies and questions used, model token counts, estimated service cost, timestamps, and technical error information needed to operate, secure, and meter the service.
  • Optional marketing consent: if you actively opt in, your email may be sent to Mailchimp for BASE1520 product and launch messages. Account creation alone does not opt you into marketing.

Before the app sends a study request, it explains that the Scripture reference, Bible text, and content needed to fulfill the request may be processed by Anthropic's API and asks for permission. Questions sent through Ask or a specialist agent also include the question and recent conversation needed to answer it. Manuscripts and local sermon recordings are not sent to Anthropic merely because they exist in the workspace. Resend processes the email address and one-time code needed to deliver transactional account-recovery messages. Hosting, database, app-store, payment, Bible-text, marketing-email, and support providers process only the information needed for their role. BASE1520 does not sell personal data and does not use Operator data for cross-company advertising tracking.

The Operator is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

You may delete an Operator account in the app or use the web request path at base1520.com/operator/account-deletion. Deletion removes account-linked studies, notes, devices, recovery records, and email records. BASE1520 may retain anonymized cost records and keyed one-way anti-abuse values that cannot be read back as an email address or device ID, plus limited records required for fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, or law.
