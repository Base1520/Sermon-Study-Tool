The C.O.V.E.N.A.N.T. method

Eight steps. One disciplined path through any passage.

Each step forces a necessary question before you move forward. The method slows you down where care matters so you can move with confidence when it is time to teach.

C

Contextualize historically & literarily

Enter the author's world and locate the passage inside its book.

O

Observe the covenant narrative

Find the passage inside God's unfolding redemptive storyline.

V

Verify authorial intent

State what the author meant and show how the text supports it.

E

Examine genre in redemptive history

Read poetry, narrative, prophecy, wisdom, and epistle on their own terms.

N

Name the faithfulness of God

Identify what the passage reveals about God's character and covenant action.

A

Anchor every text in Christ

Follow the passage's natural place in the story that reaches its climax in Jesus.

N

Navigate tensions with integrity

Handle hard texts and genuine interpretive tensions without pretending certainty.

T

Teach for transformation

Move from accurate meaning to specific, Spirit-dependent obedience and worship.

No. It prepares the preacher and teacher; it does not impersonate them. The aim is to help you see what the text means and communicate it faithfully.
